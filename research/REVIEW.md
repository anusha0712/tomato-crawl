# Tomato Crawl — candidate review sheet

Vegetarian tomato items at NYC counters, for checking by hand. Every row carries a verbatim
caption or menu line, a date, and a link. Click through and tick.

**Rules applied:** vegetarian only (no meat, no fish) · no pizza · no sit-down restaurants · no
grocery or packaged · seasonal specials preferred over permanent fixtures.

Diet is tagged per item because "vegetarian" has an ambiguous edge — most tomato pastries carry
dairy or egg. Filter to taste.

Last updated **2026-09-09**
